synopsis

Jude was only seven years old when her parents were brutally murdered and she and her sisters were taken to live in the treacherous Fairy Realm. Ten years later, all Jude wants is to fit in, even though she’s a mortal girl. But all the faeries seem to despise humans… Especially Prince Cardan, the youngest and most wicked of the Great King of Elfhame’s sons.

To win her long-desired place at Court, Jude must challenge the prince—and face the consequences of her actions.

The girl then becomes increasingly entangled in the palace’s games and intrigues, and ends up discovering her own knack for trickery and bloodshed. But when a betrayal threatens to drown the Fairy Kingdom in violence, Jude will have to risk everything in a dangerous alliance to save her sisters—and Elfhame itself. Surrounded by lies and people who wish to destroy her, Jude will have to discover the true meaning of the word “power” before it’s too late.

straight to hogwarts

“Happiness can be found, even in the darkest of times, if one only remembers to turn on the light.”

- Albus Dumbledore

synopsis

Harry Potter is a boy whose parents, both wizards, were murdered by a very powerful wizard when he was still a baby. He was then taken to live with his aunt and uncle, who had nothing to do with the supernatural. Quite the opposite. Until he was 10, Harry was a bit of a Cinderella: mistreated by his aunt and uncle, he inherited old clothes from his chubby cousin, wore patched-up glasses, and was treated like a nuisance.

On his 11th birthday, however, he seems to slip down a bottomless hole, like Alice’s in Alice in Wonderland, which leads him to a magical world. He discovers his true history and his destiny: to be a wizard’s apprentice until the day he must face the worst force of evil, the man who murdered his parents. The green-eyed, skinny, and awkward boy, so accustomed to rejection, also discovers that he is a hero in the wizarding world.

Potter learns that he is the only person to have survived an attack by that evil wizard, and that is the cause of the lightning-shaped scar he bears on his forehead. He is not just any boy; he is not even just any wizard—he is Harry Potter, a symbol of power and resilience, and a natural leader among the supernatural. The tale, filled with ghosts, talking walls, cauldrons, toads, unicorns, dragons, and giants, is not, however, merely a pastime.
